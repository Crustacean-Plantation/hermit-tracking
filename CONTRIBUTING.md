# Contributing

Comment on an open issue, or open a new one if your idea does not fit.

- Methods, sketches, and bill-of-materials notes are welcome.
- Do not commit raw coordinates, donor names, or Apple account data.
- Do not propose adhesives on the animal, or any mount the crab cannot leave by changing shells.
- Small pull requests against `docs/` are fine. Hardware builds should start as an issue so the nonprofit can say yes before anyone deploys a tag.

Crustacean Plantation Inc. keeps field decisions. A merged design is not permission to tag a wild crab.
